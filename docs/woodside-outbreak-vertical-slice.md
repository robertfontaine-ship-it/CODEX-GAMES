# WRS Quest: Woodside Outbreak — Vertical Slice

## Locked identity

A funny, arcade-style, high-pressure zombie survival game set at Woodside High School. Workplace Readiness Skills affect survival outcomes, routes, allies, equipment, and character progression instead of appearing as disconnected quiz content.

## Canonical geography

- Woodside is organized by A House, B House, and C House.
- A202 is the upstairs Marketing classroom in A House.
- A202 is the opening tutorial room and recurring player headquarters.
- B House is the central chaos zone where the largest crowds, hall-change surges, and recurring horde events occur.
- C House is a later strategic zone with fewer but more difficult threats and higher-stakes decisions.

## Real-time campaign clock

Normal-day schedule anchors:

- 7:10 — First block begins
- 8:15 — First block ends
- 8:20 — Second/third block begins
- 10:00 — Second/third block ends
- 10:05 — Lunch/class rotation begins
- 12:15 — Sixth/seventh block begins
- 1:50 — School day ends

Mission 1 occurs during first block and ends at the 8:15 bell. The 8:20 hall change triggers the first major B House horde and serves as the Mission 2 hook.

## Character creation

The player creates a persistent survivor before Mission 1.

Customization:

- Display name or gamer tag
- Avatar body and skin options
- Hair/headwear
- Top, bottoms, and shoes
- Backpack style
- Starter color accent

Starting roles:

### Marketing
- Workplace perk: gains extra Reputation from strong communication and audience decisions.
- Action ability: Hype Train briefly increases ally speed and reduces Panic.

### Tech
- Workplace perk: completes repair and information-security tasks faster.
- Action ability: EMP Burst stuns nearby threats and unlocks selected electronic routes.

### Sports Marketing
- Workplace perk: gains bonuses during teamwork, event, and promotion tasks.
- Action ability: Clutch Play provides a short sprint and stamina burst.

### Entrepreneurship
- Workplace perk: finds extra supplies and earns more value from limited resources.
- Action ability: Resourceful converts collected junk into a temporary gadget.

### Fashion
- Workplace perk: gains bonuses from visual judgment, presentation, and customer-profile tasks.
- Action ability: Quick Change breaks one grab and briefly lowers enemy detection.

### Operations
- Workplace perk: gains bonuses during safety, logistics, and prioritization tasks.
- Action ability: Fortify deploys a short-lived barricade.

No role is permanently locked. After Mission 1, performance unlocks a specialization recommendation while preserving player choice.

## A202 headquarters

A202 begins as a normal classroom and becomes the first headquarters.

Interactive stations:

- Teacher desk: mission board and campaign clock
- Student computer bank: workplace tasks, records, and information-security missions
- Supply cabinet: inventory and crafting
- Whiteboard: objectives, skill progress, and class leaderboard
- Backpack rack: loadout selection
- Locked interior door: later headquarters expansion
- Windows/hallway sightline: escalating visual threat indicator

Headquarters progression:

1. Tutorial room
2. Barricaded safe room
3. Communications hub
4. Equipment workshop
5. Survivor command center

## Mission 1 — Outbreak in A House

### Goal

Escape A202, cross upstairs A House, reach the central hub, and establish the first safe route before the 8:15 bell.

### Target length

8–12 minutes for a first playthrough; 5–7 minutes on replay.

### Sequence

#### 1. 7:10 — First Block

- Title card: `WOODSide — 7:10 AM`
- Character creation occurs at a classroom desk.
- Player selects a role and starter backpack.
- Micro-tutorial teaches movement, interaction, and the mission HUD.

#### 2. 7:26 — Conflicting Messages

A202 receives three messages:

- Office: remain in place until further notice.
- Coworker/student: there is a fight or medical problem downstairs.
- Unverified group message: leave through the nearest exit immediately.

Work task: identify the reliable instruction, protect private information, and send a concise response.

Strong outcome:
- +Trust
- Security Shield modifier
- Lower starting Panic

Weak outcome:
- Confused NPC movement
- Reduced hallway visibility

Dangerous outcome:
- A follower opens the classroom door early
- Additional zombie enters the tutorial encounter

#### 3. 7:31 — Barricade A202

Timed preparation task:

- Choose three useful objects from six.
- Place them at the door, windows, or supply station.
- Objects have visible tradeoffs between defense, speed, and future supplies.

This teaches prioritization rather than a single obvious answer.

#### 4. 7:35 — First Contact

A zombie reaches the classroom doorway.

- Player learns dodge, shove, and role ability.
- Combat is non-graphic, exaggerated, and arcade-like.
- Starter office weapons include stapler launcher, rolling-chair bash, textbook toss, and fire-extinguisher burst.

#### 5. A202 to A-House Stairwell — Hallway Run

Three-lane runner sequence using swipe or keyboard controls:

- Move lanes
- Jump backpacks and fallen signs
- Slide under partially closed gates
- Dodge slow zombies and panicked survivors
- Collect batteries, first aid, and Wolverine Tokens

Work-task consequences alter the route:

- Security Shield blocks one collision.
- Open-door mistake adds a pursuing zombie.
- Strong communication reveals safe-lane indicators.

#### 6. Stairwell Decision

The player hears two calls:

- A trapped student near the upper landing
- A staff member requesting immediate delivery of an access badge downstairs

The player chooses rescue, objective, or split-plan approach. Each choice changes allies, time, and the central-hub encounter.

#### 7. Central Hub Holdout

Survivor-style arena sequence with an on-screen joystick.

- Survive for 45 seconds while the security doors cycle.
- Auto-attacks depend on the selected role and collected equipment.
- Environmental interactions include rolling carts, extinguisher clouds, door magnets, and PA distractions.
- Panic increases when surrounded and decreases after clean dodges, rescues, or role abilities.

#### 8. Workplace Challenge — Emergency Access

The player must review limited information and choose the correct order for securing:

- Communication access
- Medical/safety access
- Student/staff movement routes
- Confidential records

Options create clear gameplay consequences rather than only correct/incorrect feedback.

#### 9. 8:15 — The Bell

- The school bell rings at the exact end of first block.
- Lights and PA audio briefly stabilize.
- A202 becomes the official headquarters.
- Radio warning: B House is filling as students prepare for the 8:20 transition.
- Final shot shows movement building in the central B House corridors.

Mission complete screen:

- Survivors rescued
- Trust
- Panic control
- Workplace competencies demonstrated
- Route choices
- Supplies collected
- Zombie Report unlocked
- Recommended specialization

## Mission 2 hook — Five Minutes to B House

The player has five in-world minutes before the 8:20 hall-change surge.

Potential objectives:

- Restore communications before the halls fill
- Reach a stranded survivor team in B House
- Secure a safer route between A House and the front office

At 8:20, a mandatory B House Horde Event begins regardless of the chosen objective.

## Campaign map

### Act I — A House: First Response

2. Five Minutes to B House

Purpose: tutorial, character identity, first allies, and headquarters formation.

### Act II — B House: Ground Zero

5. Hall-Change Horde
6. B-House Blackout
7. Lunch Wave
8. The Middle Stairwell
9. Retrieve the Records

Purpose: recurring chaos zone, random horde events, route changes, and the campaign's most recognizable location.

### Act III — Shared Facilities

11. Cafeteria Rush
12. Gym Lock-In
13. Auditorium After Dark
14. Front-Office Siege

Purpose: distinct arcade formats and stronger WRS integration through customer service, safety, teamwork, technology, and logistics.

### Act IV — C House: Resistance

15. C-House Signal
16. Quiet Hall
17. Power and Priorities
18. C-House Command

Purpose: more strategic enemies, difficult choices, and fewer obvious solutions.

### Act V — Exterior Campus

19. Parking-Lot Extraction
20. Bus-Loop Breakdown
21. Fields of Woodside
22. Woodside Park Route

Purpose: open arenas, escort missions, vehicle/lane sequences, and resource scarcity.

### Finale — Return to A202

23. The Last Bell

The player returns to the original classroom for a final defense and uses accumulated workplace skills, allies, upgrades, and earlier route decisions.
