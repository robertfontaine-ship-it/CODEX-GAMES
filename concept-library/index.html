Concept Hub 01

PROMOTION

Promotion is how a business communicates with customers to inform, persuade, remind, and build relationships. It is one part of the marketing mix and should be shaped by the target market.

Why am I learning this?

Businesses need attention — but the right kind.

Promotion helps a business decide what message to send, which audience should receive it, what channel should carry it, and what action the customer should take.

Micro Mission

Placement Hunter

You work for a new basketball shoe brand. Choose one movie, show, game, creator, team, or event where product placement would make sense.

  1. Name the placement opportunity.
  2. Describe the audience.
  3. Explain why the placement fits the brand.
  4. Predict one benefit for the company.
Hub Mission

Launch the Drop

A new sneaker company has $50,000 to launch a basketball shoe. Build a promotional mix using at least three promotional tools.

  1. Identify the target market.
  2. Select at least three promotional methods.
  3. Explain why each method fits the audience.
  4. Allocate the $50,000 budget.
  5. State how you would measure success.
Common Mistake

Promotion and advertising are not the same thing. Advertising is one tool inside the larger promotion mix.
